Comparison Desk · Pitch Black vs Chaos Rising

Chaos Rising is holding value better than Pitch Black
Index 93.17 vs 84.72 since Aug 24

Both Mega Evolution sets got their own sold-basis index on Aug 24, 2026, at 100.00. By the Sep 21 mark Chaos Rising sat at 93.17 (down 6.8%) and Pitch Black at 84.72 (down 15.3%), and Pitch Black lost 6.4% in the latest week alone. Pitch Black still owns the biggest single card: Mega Darkrai ex SIR at $182.25 (median of 30 sales, Sep 22–26) against Mega Greninja ex SIR at $155.00 (30 sales, Sep 23–26). Sealed, the ETBs sit a few dollars apart (lowest asks $67.00 vs $70.50 on Sep 26), while Pitch Black booster boxes are ten times as available on eBay (40 live vs 4).

2 · What This Means

Read it as a holder, not a ripper

The index gap is the whole case for Chaos Rising. Same start date, same method, same 36-card basket size, and one set has given back roughly twice as much as the other in five weeks. Chaos Rising is also two months older, so more of its post-release supply has already been absorbed; Pitch Black is still in the window where sealed product keeps arriving, and 40 live booster-box listings against 4 says the same thing from the sealed side.

The case for Pitch Black is one card. Mega Darkrai ex SIR is the highest single sold comp in either set at $182.25, and the two Darkrai chase cards together clear the two Greninja cards by about $32. If you are buying a card rather than a set, that is the row that matters, and it is the row Pitch Black wins.

What the tape does not say. Five weekly marks is a short history, and neither index has a since-release series; both begin Aug 24. The sealed figures are asks, not sales, so they tell you where sellers are, not where boxes clear. A single week can reverse a 1.8% move; it is less likely to reverse a 15.3% one, but the next Monday mark is the test, and it goes on the index pages whether it helps this verdict or not.

If the question is which set to hold as sealed product, the tape favors Chaos Rising on price stability and scarcity. If the question is which set has the card worth chasing, it is Pitch Black's Darkrai. Both index pages carry the full 36-card baskets: Chaos Rising Chase Index and Pitch Black Chase Index.
