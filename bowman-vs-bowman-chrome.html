Comparison Desk · 2026 Bowman vs 2026 Bowman Chrome

May's Bowman 1st autos held their price while Bowman Chrome's fell 14.1% in six days
Index 76.88 vs 85.87 at the Oct 1 mark

Both boxes are opened for the same thing: 1st Bowman Chrome Prospect Autographs. 2026 Bowman came out May 13 and mixes paper cards with Chrome prospects; 2026 Bowman Chrome came out Sep 9 and is all Chrome. The paper cards are not what collectors chase. We price every 1st auto in each release from sold comps. From the Sep 25 mark to the Oct 1 mark, Bowman's autos moved −0.3% and Bowman Chrome's fell 14.1%. Bowman also has the bigger names right now: its top auto sells for $153.35, Chrome's for $47.00. Chrome is the cheaper way to an auto from a sealed box: two autos for a lowest ask of $515 against one auto for $385 in Bowman (Oct 3 asks, not sales).

2 · What This Means

Same chase, two different moments

Bowman Chrome is in its first weeks, and new releases usually give back. Its index fell 14.1% between Sep 25 and Oct 1, most of it in the last three days. Bowman's May autos are five months past release and barely moved over the same six days. That is the plainest thing the tape says: right now the May class is steady and the September class is still finding its price.

The big names are in May's Bowman. Andrew Fischer, Eric Hartman and Ethan Holliday each sell for more than double Chrome's best priced auto. Chrome's headline 1sts (Wandy Asigen, Angeibel Gomez, Francisco Renteria, Luis Hernandez) are not in its index yet because they have too few sales to price. When they enter, Chrome's top rows could change.

Most autos in either box are small. The middle 1st auto sells for about $9 in both, and more than half of each priced list is under $10. A hobby box is a ticket to a short list of names. Chrome gives you two tickets for a lower ask per auto; Bowman gives you one ticket to the class with the bigger names.

What the tape does not say. Each index has three live marks, Sep 25 to Oct 1, so this is a six-day read, not a trend. The two levels sit on different bases (May and September), so compare the moves on the same dates, not 76.88 against 85.87 as if they were one scale. Box figures are asks. The next marks land on BB26 and BCB26 whether they help this page or not. For every 1st auto across both releases in one basket, see the 2026 1st Bowman Chrome Auto Index; for the Chrome checklist and box math, the 2026 Bowman Chrome guide.
