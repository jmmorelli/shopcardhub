Legend & Methodology

SCOPE — the secret tier: all 36 beyond-base slots (#85-120). Universe = 36; the basket = every card with a sourced sold read. Pokémon pull rates differ across rarity tiers, so no equal-supply claim is made — this is a price-weighted chase-basket index, plainly. · WEIGHT — price ÷ basket value (priced cards only). · PRICE — sold comps only, dated per row; never asks, never blended, never estimated. · FULL METHODOLOGY — solds vs asks vs engine marks vs proxies, the one-page read: /how-prices-work · INDEX LEVEL — basket value ÷ divisor (10.640652), set so that 100 = Jul 2026 (release month): the level IS the cumulative return since then. Rebased Sep 30, 2026 from the original Aug 24 launch base (every level × 0.8233; shape and % moves unchanged). Months before the live marks are reconstructed from PriceCharting's monthly ungraded price history with today's basket, labelled on the chart. The divisor adjusts (and is logged) whenever the basket composition changes, so only prices move the level. · ★ — Track in your Vault (free, no account): Hunting or I Own It. · NO CALLS — ShopCardHub retired Pokémon price calls on Aug 18, 2026 (graded record: no edge). An index is a measurement, not a recommendation.
