How we think about cards. A card is not a stock. You can't sell it in a second, the spread is wide, and a prospect's best moment usually arrives without warning — a call-up on a Tuesday and the card is gone by Thursday. So we don't try to time it. We look for cards we'd be glad to own a year from now, buy them at a price that makes sense today, and let the player do the rest. Every call we publish is graded at 6 and 12 months on the Scorecard, and nothing is ever deleted.
