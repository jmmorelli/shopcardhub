Method. One set, one index. The universe is every card in the set; the basket is the cards that trade as ungraded singles — at least 6 clean single-card sold comps in the trailing 30 days to enter, 4 to stay. Price-weighted on PriceCharting's dated ungraded sold list (blended eBay + TCGplayer), never asks. Caps: no card above 25% and positions above 5% never past 50% together (the Select Sector SPDR 5/50 rule) — a weight marked * is capped, and every cap is a weight in the divisor math, so applying one never moves the level. Level = Σ(sold mark × weight) ÷ divisor 47.9623; entries, exits and cap changes are logged divisor adjustments; reconstitution quarterly (first Monday of Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct, announced the Monday before). 0 of 191 cards are in the universe but not the basket. n30 is a liquidity gate, never a volume figure (the source caps its table at 60 rows). Includes the Classic Collection reprints (Charizard #4, Lugia #149 …) — they are in the set on PriceCharting's listing and enter the basket the week they clear the screen; the Ultra-Premium Collection that carries them ships Nov 6. Product waves run through Dec 4; the index is inception-forward, so supply arriving later is a market event, never a restatement. Bid buttons are live eBay auctions on the exact card (verified title, soonest close with bids first, refreshed every 15 minutes) — bids, not marks. An index is a measurement, not a call. What You're Actually Hunting

guide
The Chase
⚡ THE HEADLINE MECHANIC
30 Pikachu Variants — One Per Pack
Every booster guarantees one of 30 unique Pikachu cards. That's a built-in floor for pack EV and a completionist set-within-a-set — expect a "master Pikachu set" market to form immediately. The rarest variants will separate from the pack fast; watch early sold data before paying up for any single variant.
🔥 THE VALUE DRIVER
Classic Collection — 30 Reprints, Incl. Base Set Charizard
Thirty reprinted cards spanning Base Set to modern — headlined by Charizard. Available two ways: dedicated 3-card Classic packs only inside the $179.99 Ultra-Premium Collections, or as rare pulls in regular boosters. The Charizard reprint is this set's single biggest demand magnet. Precedent worth knowing: Celebrations 2021's Classic Collection reprints held value far better than that set's base cards.
🧬 SECRET RARES
Mewtwo ex 157/128 & Mew ex 158/128
The two "futuristic rare" secret cards top the standard checklist. Both also anchor $29.99 Figure Collections in the Nov 6 wave — promo versions there, chase versions in packs. Wait for sold comps before setting targets; pre-release hype pricing on secrets is historically 30–50% above the 30-day post-launch settle.
🔤 THE SLEEPER
Mini Tin "ANNIVERSARY" Art Cards
Ten Unown art cards across the Day & Night mini tins collectively spell ANNIVERSARY. Cheap ($9.99/tin), a natural completionist target, and exactly the kind of niche insert that quietly 3–5x's when a set is beloved. Low-cost lottery ticket, not a plan.

30th Celebration — Pass On These

✗
Any listing above MSRP — Waves keep dropping and stock is holding up better than Pitch Black or Prismatic did. The scalper premium evaporates at each restock. List price or wait.

Sealed as a long hold — Anniversary sets get printed to meet demand (Celebrations 2021 was reprinted into oblivion and sealed compressed for a year). An all-holo mass-market celebration set is built for volume. Hold the right singles and skip the boxes.

Key Factors That Will Move Prices

guide
Risk Assessment
⬛ HIGH IMPACT
Print Run — the Celebrations 2021 Precedent
The 25th-anniversary set was reprinted continuously for a year and sealed EV collapsed. TPC has every incentive to do it again. Watch restock cadence at Pokémon Center and Fanatics through October — fast, deep restocks mean treat everything as an opening product, not an investment.
⬛ HIGH IMPACT
Classic Collection Pull Rates in Regular Packs
Reprints as "rare chase cards" in standard boosters — at unpublished odds. If the Charizard reprint pulls at Umbreon-SIR-style rates (~1:1,400), its single price runs to several hundred dollars; if it's merely uncommon, the UPC exclusivity story weakens. First-week rip data settles this.
◽ MEDIUM
Capital Rotation Out of the Mega Block
Anniversary hype pulls collector dollars from Pitch Black and the rest of the 2026 Mega sets through Q4. If you're holding Mega-era SIRs for a flip, the pre–Sep 16 window is when attention (and liquidity) is still on them.
◽ MEDIUM
Master Pikachu Set Economics
30 variants at 1-per-pack means variant rarity tiers decide everything. If some variants are short-printed, completionists will chase them at any price — and the commons go to bulk. Don't buy variant singles until the tier structure is visible in sold data.
