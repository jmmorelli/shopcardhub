# Mo's own voice — verbatim samples from @shopcardhub (Rule 0 corpus)

Read before writing ANY post, reply draft, or Tape line. These are Mo's own words on the account, copied exactly. Every lane appends new ones (newest first, date, context, verbatim) — the X daily lane already logs voice samples; they go here too. Never paraphrase a sample. Never store his bookmarks.

## What the samples have in common (CoS read, Oct 14 2026)
- He reacts to one thing. He never summarizes.
- Often starts lowercase, mid-thought ("makes no sense.", "personally,", "straight from a pack…").
- Numbers almost never appear. When they do it is one, and it is about his own card or order, not a market.
- Collector shorthand: "zard", "gold zard", "card saver", "slab", "rip".
- His own actions are the content: what he is sending in, what he cracked, what he will use from now on.
- A photo or GIF does the talking when there is nothing to say ("No love?" + a photo; the "one of us" GIF).
- Short questions, open and real ("No love?", "How is that even possible?" is the kind he answers).
- Warm and plain with other collectors ("Love a good Topsun deep dive. Great thread!", "Same.", "And the beaver alums 🔥").
- One emoji at most, usually none.
- He does not link the site in replies. Ever.

## The test for anything the CoS writes
Could Mo have typed this on his phone in the time it takes to wait for coffee? If it has a date range, a ticker, three percentages, or reads like a terminal, the answer is no. Rewrite or hand him the image and the one fact and let him write it.
