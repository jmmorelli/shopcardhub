**Title:** Started tracking a few sets right before the dip. Should I reset the start date, or just leave it?

Kind of a nerdy question for the price-tracking people here.

In late August I started tracking the chase cards (SIR/IR/UR) from the newer sets as a simple index. Each set starts at 100 and moves with sold prices of raw copies. Pretty much right after I started, everything slid:

- Pitch Black: 82.8
- Chaos Rising: 85.3
- Destined Rivals: 87.7
- Ascended Heroes: 88.5
- Prismatic: 93.6

Now I'm second-guessing the setup. Does starting it right before a dip make the whole thing look worse than it really is? Or is "start the day you start and never touch it" the honest way to do it, even if the timing was bad?

It might be doable for sets from the last 3–5 years, but then half the sets would start at release and the other half wouldn't, and I'm not sure that's better.

For comparison, I started 151 last week and it's sitting at 99.3, so that one looks flat mostly because it's brand new, not necessarily because 151 is holding better.

Heads up that the site is very much a work in progress, and I'm not really a web developer. I started it so my kids and I could easily figure out which Pokémon and baseball cards to look for, and it's turned into a fun project we work on together. So I'm really just looking for input from people who know this stuff better than I do.
