## CoS reading (why the thesis holds, and where it needs help)
1. **The EPN click window makes retention the mechanism, not a nicety.** EPN pays on purchases inside a short window after the click. A buyer of a $1k+ card checks the price many times over weeks; the site earns only if it is the last click when they decide. A tracker they return to is the only reliable way to be the last click. The Sep 4 read shows the shape of that click: a specific listing at a shown price, 39% conversion; the $1,950 PCA buyer clicked and bought within 90 minutes.
2. **Arithmetic:** ~$300/mo at ~3.5% ≈ $8.5k/mo GMV through our links. The Aug 20–Sep 2 window did $2.16k. The gap closes with ~10–15 high-end purchases a month, not with hundreds of $20 orders. EPN revenue is a heavy-tailed sum — a handful of large purchases dominate — so the site is designed for the tail buyer, not the median visitor. This amends the Sep 3 "traffic first" weighting: the *right* ~50 returning people outrank 10x random traffic; traffic is how we find them, polish is still not the lever.
3. **Baseline (GA4):** Aug 25 read: 837 first_visit / 838 users, Vault used by 5 people in 28 days. **Sep 4 read (Aug 6–Sep 2): 61 returning / 921 new = 6.2% returning.** The site acquires and does not retain. Mo's thesis is a diagnosis that matches the data.
4. **Credibility gate:** the high-end buyer already has 130point, eBay solds, PriceCharting, Card Ladder. They return for a number nobody else publishes, with the method shown. Today's engine number is a verified ASK floor; that audience knows the difference. The hammer capture (`049180a`, Sep 3) is what turns it into a two-sided quote (ask floor / last hammer / spread). Brand promise until hammers mature: "the exact card, verified" — not "fair value." Never oversell to the audience most able to catch it.
5. **Retention unit ≠ index page ≠ Vault.** The set index is the front door (and SEO) — Mo's "table"; the thing a chaser bookmarks is the single card's chart page (nightly series, ask floor, hammers when available, alert, **and the live listings at shown prices — the link type that actually converts**); the Vault is the account layer people adopt after they trust the number. Sequence: index → card page → Vault. The Vault "struggles" because it was being asked to be the hook. A blank Vault asks a visitor to know what they want; an index shows them 70 priced cards with ★ Track on every row.
6. **Tier gap to watch:** the $1k+ purchase is a numbered parallel or graded auto (the PCA Orange /25 proves it) — one tier above what the BASE index measures. The index brings them in; the card page must show the tier they actually buy (raw base / PSA 10 / key parallels for that name). For Bowman the high-end buyer's table is the AUTOS tab — BOW26 ships with BASE + AUTOS from day one.
7. **Phone first, for real.** Both buyers in the window were on phones, and phone clicks are the ones EPN can't attribute to a page. Card pages and index rows must be built for a thumb: the live listing with the number is the tap target, above the fold.

## Bowman indices — decision
- **Per set**, as Mo says. This is also what was locked Aug 24 (`set-index-etf`): each Bowman-family set page gets BASE (top-100 base Chrome 1sts, price-weighted) | AUTOS (1st Chrome base autos, 15% single-card cap at reconstitution) | PARALLELS (display only). Within a set the constituents *are* the 1sts — "per set" vs "1sts" is not a conflict; what Mo is rejecting is a cross-set 1sts basket, which would be a rebalanced-by-feel thematic with survivorship bias. Frozen checklist = fixed constituents = clean divisor math.
- **Mark basis under the new regime:** activate on the engine's verified ask floor, labeled ask-basis (spec already allows it), and restate the divisor to hammer basis when hammer coverage is adequate (target: ≥60% of basket value with a hammer inside 30 days). Level never moves on a basis change — divisor restatement, logged.
- **Order:** BOW26 first (engine already tracks the board names; paper marks until Chrome streets), BCB26 after the Sep 9–10 release has ~3–4 weeks of tape, SAPH26 after that, DRAFT26 in Dec.
- Publish concentration next to the level (top-5 weight, effective holdings, skew/kurtosis) — Bowman baskets will be far more concentrated than the Pokémon ones; that is a feature the audience understands.
