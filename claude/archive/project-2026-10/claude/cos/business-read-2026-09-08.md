## What this means for the Sep 4 strategy
The retention thesis (index → card chart page → Vault, for high-end chasers arriving from X) is built for an audience that is not arriving, entering through pages nobody lands on. The audience that IS arriving, via Bing, searched "<set> release date / what to buy" or "<player> rookie cards", and clicks eBay listings from player pages. The engine's unique data is the right retention asset — but it is on the wrong URLs. Retention for the real audience = the release guide and the player page carrying the engine's nightly number, the hammer reads, the live listings at shown prices, and the alert capture — on the page that already ranks, not on a new `/card-*` page Google has not indexed.
