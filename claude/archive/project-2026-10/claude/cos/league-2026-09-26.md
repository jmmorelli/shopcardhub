## The idea, in Mo's words and what changed

Mo asked for a team of bots that find growth avenues on their own, compete, and get culled and cloned on results, with the CoS overseeing. Kept: fixed slate, one scoring date, cull on a pre-committed rule, clone the winner's method. Changed: the unit of competition is a **search page** (the one channel a bot can work end to end without a human account — Reddit/forums/DMs ban bots and would cost the domain), the three agents run **different theses** rather than clones of one prompt (clones only measure luck), and R14's two reads apply — floor culls, only a ranking read (≥ 100 organic sessions per agent) breeds. At today's traffic a 3-vs-7-click split is Poisson noise; firing on it culls the unlucky.
