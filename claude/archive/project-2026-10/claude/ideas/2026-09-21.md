## Idea 34 — Price every verified listing against the mark it sits under: a "−12% vs mark" chip on the engine block's listing rail

1. **Title.** Per-listing delta-vs-mark chip on the engine block's verified fixed-price listings, with under-mark clicks routed to their own `-bm` custom ID.
2. **Mechanism.** `js/engine-block.js` already renders, for every engine card, the nightly mark (`c.last`, dated) and up to 8 verified fixed-price listings sorted by price + shipping, each deep-linked with `customid=card-<id>`. Step 4 of `render()` gains one computed span per fixed row: `((total − mark) / mark)` as a signed percentage, e.g. **"−12% vs Sep 20 mark"** or **"+38% vs mark"**, tabular, neutral colour, no adjective — never "deal", "undervalued", "cheap". It is shown only when both numbers exist and the mark is not gated; **auction rows get nothing** (a current bid is a 12-hour-lagged floor — STATE Sep 16 retraction — and a "−60% vs mark" chip on a bid would be exactly the false claim that retraction removed). Rows whose total sits under the mark have the `customid` in their eBay URL rewritten from `card-<id>` to `card-<id>-bm` so the EPN split is readable against the plain rows. The chart's stat band already prints ask Q1–Q3 and the basis label, so the reader can see what "mark" means two lines up; the chip carries the mark's date so a stale feed reads as stale.
3. **Site synthesis.** The nightly engine's marks (`prices-latest.json`), the engine blocks (8 hosts + the `/card-*` pages, 34+ cards, and every page #30 adds), `/api/comps` card mode, per-listing EPN custom IDs. No new page, no new query, no server change.
4. **Evidence.** Internal, dated: listing-level links converted at **39%** on the Sep 4 EPN read while static search links converted to four sub-$3 sales (gap analysis G3); the only two sales that mattered since Aug 25 were **$1,950** and **$860** singles, both on phones (§6); and on the Sep 4–17 Custom-ID read **every engine-hosted ID earned $0** on ~10 clicks (ledger #30) — the rail gets clicks and converts none, which is the row this idea is aimed at. External precedent: eBay itself puts a relative-price signal on listings ("Great Price" badge, automatic, seller-side — https://www.ebay.com/sellercenter/ebay-for-business/great-price-badge) — **precedent only; eBay publishes no effect size**, and no other external evidence on the conversion effect of a relative-price chip was found. Reasoning only on the size.
5. **Money math.** Lever: **$/click** — it changes nothing about how many people click, only which listing they click and whether the click ends in a purchase, on the highest-ticket links the site has. ESTIMATE **+$0–$10/mo at today's engine-rail clicks** (~5/week sitewide; a zero month is the normal outcome — G8) and **+$10–$40/mo once #30 is live** (assumption: the star-page rails carry $400–$900 cards, and the chip turns one extra rail click into a sale every one to two months at ~3% of GMV). Rung: **M0** contributor; an **M1** mechanism only in combination with #30, which is why the read is designed as `-bm` vs plain rather than "did revenue go up".
6. **Cost and Mo-time.** $0. One renderer edit, client-side, gate-neutral (no price cell is written — the chip is arithmetic on two figures already on the page; `audit-prices` check 8 `ask-in-price-slot` should be run to confirm the chip's markup does not land inside a price slot). Mo-time: 0.
7. **First step.** One Wednesday build session: (a) confirm the Browse API's `itemWebUrl` carries `customid=` as a rewritable query param (it is set server-side via `X-EBAY-C-ENDUSERCTX affiliateReferenceId`; if the returned URL does not expose it, the split is done with `/api/comps?customid=card-<id>-bm` on a second call, or the chip ships without the split and the read is GA4 `click{kind}` only — say which); (b) add the span + the rewrite in `js/engine-block.js` step 4, fixed rows only, JS_V bump, 8 hosts regenerated; (c) phone check at 390px on a host with an open chart (Mo's Sep 18 "too busy" rule — one short chip on the price column, no new row); (d) three gates; (e) verify live on `ethan-holliday-rookie-cards` that the chip's arithmetic matches the band's mark and the row's price + shipping to the dollar.

Earlier kill: any chip rendered on an auction row, or on a gated mark, is a defect and the chip comes off that host the same day.
