### ⚠ FLAG (not an idea — the CoS owes a same-day ruling): 254 per-card eBay links on the six Pokémon index pages send NO affiliate parameters to eBay

Found while measuring idea 36, verified on the **live** `/prismatic-evolutions-index` at 04:24 PT (49 of 49 rows) and in the clone on all six pages (AH26 38 · CR26 36 · DR25 59 · PF25 36 · PB26 36 · PRIS25 49 = **254**). Each popup "Live Listings →" URL is built with the card number unencoded:

`…/sch/i.html?_nkw=pokemon+Umbreon+ex+#161&LH_BIN=1&mkcid=1&…&campid=5339155990&toolid=10001&customid=pris25-index`

A browser treats the first `#` as the fragment delimiter, so **what eBay receives is `_nkw=pokemon+Umbreon+ex+` — no card number, no `LH_BIN`, no `mkevt`, no `campid`, no `customid`.** Every click through those 254 links since they shipped has been an untracked, uncredited eBay search for the wrong (broader) query. `audit-site` reads the `href` attribute, sees `campid=` present, and passes it — the gate-reads-structure class again. The BCB26 builder, the buy strips, the sealed rows, the card pages and the engine blocks all `encodeURIComponent` the query and are not affected (checked: no other page carries a `#` inside `_nkw`). **Fix is one line per generator/array (`%23` or drop the `#`); it is compliance hygiene under Mo's Sep 21 "I do not want to lose EPN", and it belongs to the CoS desk today, not to the Wednesday build.** The desk cannot fix, push or verify in a browser; the URL parse above is RFC 3986, not a guess.
