## Idea 46 — Give "eBay sold comps →" its own custom ID, and put it where the dated sold marks are

1. **Title.** Split the 77 existing "eBay Sold Comps →" links onto `customid=<page>-sold`, and add one "eBay sold listings →" link to each chase-strip card on the index pages.

2. **Mechanism.**
   - **(a) The split.** The site already carries **77 eBay sold-view links (`LH_Sold=1&LH_Complete=1`) on 17 pages**: 10 on `roy-watch-2026`; 5 each on the Messi, Ronaldo, Yamal, Haaland, Mbappé, Kane, Wembanyama, Flagg, Arquette and Bangers pages; 4 on Ohtani, Holliday and the Pitch Black guide; 3 on World Cup; 1 on Chrome Black. **Every one reports under the same custom ID as the Buy button beside it** (e.g. both Messi links are `customid=lionel-messi-cards`). The change routes each sold-view link to `<page>-sold` through `withCustomId()` in `tools/lib/epn.mjs`. The same pass sets `rel="noopener sponsored"`: **71 of the 77 sold-view anchors on 14 pages** lack `sponsored` (Messi: 10 of its 12 eBay anchors are `rel="noopener"` only). Nothing else changes: no copy, no position, no query.
   - **(b) The extension.** `tools/build-chase-strip.mjs` gives each chase card (3 per index page, $50+ sold marks) a small text line under the existing listing link. It reads "eBay sold listings →", is built by `ebaySearchUrl({ sold: true })` on the card's own guarded query, and uses `customid=<tk>-<num>-sold`. There is no figure.
   - **Labelling.** The label says "eBay sold listings", **not** "the sales behind this mark". Our marks are PriceCharting blends (eBay + TCGplayer), and eBay's sold view shows only eBay's own ~90 days.
   - **(c) The league pages.** The 9 league pages with sold reads (listed in item 4) are agent-owned (R27), so the desk does not touch them. Instead, one line goes into `tools/league/BRIEF.md` for agent B/C improve runs: *beside a dated mark, the citation stays and an "eBay sold listings →" link sits next to it, `customid=<slug>-sold`.*

3. **Site synthesis.** It rides on the chase strips (all Pokémon, Bowman and classic index pages), the 17 player/board pages that already carry the link, `tools/lib/epn.mjs` (whose `sold` option already exists and is unused by the generators) and the league's improve runs. No page, no nav item, no data.

4. **Evidence.**
   - **EPN pays on anything bought in 24 h, last click wins.** EPN's own page says: *"a cookie … credits your link with their activity for 24 hours … they don't need to purchase the specific item you linked to"*, and *"eBay uses last-click methodology"* (https://partnernetwork.ebay.com/solutions/step-1-understanding-cookies-commissions-and-getting-paid, fetched 2026-09-30). So a sold-view click is a full EPN click. The gap analysis §3 G3 buyer is the shape: he clicked, then found the $1,950 card himself on eBay.
   - **The comparable data site monetizes exactly this action.** A PriceCharting item page, curled 2026-09-30 ~04:30 PT (`/game/pokemon-evolving-skies/umbreon-vmax-215`), carries **383 eBay links on `campid=5338999485`**. Every completed-sale row is an affiliate link to the sold item.
   - **Our side of that leak.** In the clone, **74 `href`s to pricecharting.com / sportscardspro.com sit on 14 of our pages**. Most are the 9 league question/comparison pages: charizard-ex-worth 17, gengar-ex-worth 10, ascended-heroes-vs-destined-rivals 7, three vs-pages 6 each, moonbreon-worth 6, is-pokemon-151-worth-it 5, umbreon-ex-worth 4. A reader who verifies a mark there and clicks a sale row hands the last click to PriceCharting. That undoes our cookie if they had clicked us first. The citation must stay (honesty rule); the fix is to offer our own verification link beside it.
   - **The one sale that mattered came from a page carrying this link, and we can't tell which link.** `lionel-messi-cards`: 4 clicks → $860.52 → $25.82 (EPN Custom ID, Sep 4–17, gap analysis §6). That page pairs every Buy button with an "eBay Sold Comps →" link on the same ID.
   - **Traffic on the carrying pages (GA4 landing28, snapshot Sep 29):** Bangers 23, Haaland 19 (5 KE), Yamal 19 (6 KE), Flagg 14, Ronaldo 13, Messi 11. Surfaces (index pages, home, board, watchlist) had 548 views/28 d (`retentionSplit28`).
   - **No external evidence that a sold-view click converts at any given rate.** That part is reasoning only, and the split is what measures it.

5. **Money math.** Lever: **clicks** (more EPN-cookied clicks per investor session), measured per link type.
   - **(a) earns nothing new by itself.** It answers whether the Messi-pattern link pays, which is the precondition for (b) and for the league line.
   - **ESTIMATE for (b), +$1–$8/mo at today's traffic.** Assumptions: ~550 surface views/28 d; 1–3% of chase-strip viewers want the comps, which gives 5–16 extra eBay clicks/mo; the EPC on record is $0.30–$0.52.

   - Rung **M0**, small. At this volume one order decides a month (G8).

6. **Cost and Mo-time.** $0. Mo-time **0**. (a) is one tagged-link pass plus gates, ~20 min. (b) is one builder branch plus `--bake`, ~40 min. (c) is one line in a brief.
