## The rule (rulebook §2c, `tools/lib/holo-tier.mjs`)

- **Holo-tier = printed rarity** from `data/kb/pokemon-rarity.json` (pokemontcg.io data, `tools/kb/build-rarity.mjs`, 23 sets): Rare Holo
  and up — Shining, Crystal/secret, V/VMAX/VSTAR/GX, ultra, rainbow, illustration, special illustration, hyper, radiant, Classic
  Collection, ACE SPEC; SV/ME-era "Rare" (holofoil) counts. Common / Uncommon / non-holo Rare do not.
- Holo-tier slots are **standing constituents**: never enter or exit on the screen. Marked on the nearest window with clean sales
  (ticker window → 90 → 180 → 365 d); under the stay threshold they are flagged **thin** in the table with the window and count the
  mark came from. A holo with no clean raw sale in a year stays out and is **named** on the page.
- Non-holo slots keep the 6/4 screen unchanged. Nobody picks the holos — the printer did; the rule is mechanical and identical on every ticker.
- **90-day window for the ten WOTC Unlimited sets** (BS99 JU99 FO99 TR00 NG00 NDC01 NR01 ND02 AQ03 SK03) — Mo's Oct 6 choice; one print
  family, one window (the 1st Ed / Shadowless siblings have been on 90 since Sep 30). Kept rows re-marked on the 90-day median.
- Applied by the new `--holo` op as a **logged, level-neutral divisor adjustment** (divisorLog / capLog / screenLog + history row
  "holo rule (+N)"). Every one of the 27 levels is identical before and after. `--mark` and `--recon` honour the rule; a stored-vs-CONFIG
  window mismatch refuses a mark.
