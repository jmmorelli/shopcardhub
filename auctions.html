Every live eBay auction that passes the exact-card filter for the 20 cards the engine tracks, with the card's nightly mark and what buyers actually paid at recent auctions beside the current bid. Sorted by time to close. A bid under the mark is the one thing on eBay you can act on today; whether it stays under is up to the last ten seconds. Opening prices with no bids are shown, but marked as exactly that.

Mark = the engine's verified ask floor for the exact card (ask basis, re-marked nightly). Hammers = the median of auction closes we watched for that card, shown only after two or more closes. Bid + shipping is compared to each separately; they are never blended. Auctions that fail the exact-card filter (parallels, slabs, lots, other card codes) are not shown. Under the markCurrent bid + shipping is below the engine's ask floor for the card. Solid green = someone has actually bid. Dashed "opening" = the seller's starting price with no bids yet; anyone can be first, and most of these move in the last hour.
vs hammersThe same bid against the median of closes the engine has watched for this card. Fewer than two closes and it says "no hammers yet".
VerifiedTitle must carry the card code, the year, and none of the parallel/slab/lot words. Same filter that makes the nightly mark.
