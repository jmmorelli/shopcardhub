2026 Topps Holiday Baseball
Releases October 21, 2026

2026 Topps Holiday Baseball releases on October 21, 2026. That is the date on Topps' release calendar and on its mega-box product page. A mega box is 10 packs of 10 cards with one autograph, relic or Chrome base card, at a $29.99 list price. Topps showed the mega box sold out for pre-order on Sep 29. On eBay, the lowest single-box presale ask that day was $45.99. Past boxes are the only sold read there is: 2025 Holiday mega boxes sold for a median $39.00 across 9 sales, Aug 31–Sep 27, 2026.

What This Means

The Investor Read

At list price, the mega box has held up. A 2025 Holiday mega box cost $29.99 and sold for a $39.00 median in September 2026. Three 2024 boxes sold for a $45.00 median in late September. That is a modest gain for a box you hold a year or two, before shipping and fees.

The presale asks are above that. The lowest single-box ask on Sep 29 was $45.99. That is more than a 2025 box sells for today. Paying presale prices means paying a year's gain up front. The $29.99 list price is the number that has worked; the presale premium has not been tested yet.

Base cards are not the play. Raw Kurtz and Ohtani base cards from 2025 Holiday sell for about a dollar. A PSA 10 Kurtz sold for $53.99 on Jul 18, 2026 and $65.00 on Aug 27, 2026, so grading is where the margin was. Murakami #H82 is the 2026 card most likely to show that gap, but nobody knows its price until it sells.

What we will do. The first 2026 Holiday sold comps usually show up within two weeks of October 21. This page will get dated box and rookie medians then. Until then, every 2026 figure here is an ask or a list price.
