Method. One set, one index. The universe is every card in the set; the basket is the cards that trade as ungraded singles — at least 6 clean single-card sold comps in the trailing 30 days to enter, 4 to stay. Price-weighted on PriceCharting's dated ungraded sold list (blended eBay + TCGplayer), never asks. Caps: no card above 25% and positions above 5% never past 50% together (the Select Sector SPDR 5/50 rule) — a weight marked * is capped, and every cap is a weight in the divisor math, so applying one never moves the level. Level = Σ(sold mark × weight) ÷ divisor 11.958535; entries, exits and cap changes are logged divisor adjustments; reconstitution quarterly (first Monday of Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct, announced the Monday before). 21 of 196 cards are in the universe but not the basket. Left out of the universe on purpose: the “Pokemon Communication #152b” row — a second PriceCharting item filed under #152; Pokémon Communication #152 itself is in. n30 is a liquidity gate, never a volume figure (the source caps its table at 60 rows). The February 2019 Sun & Moon set (SM9) that introduced TAG TEAM GX cards: 181 cards plus 15 secret rares (196). The full-art TAG TEAM GX cards lead it, Latias & Latios GX #170 first. Reverse holo, Prize Pack and stamped prints are separate items and are not in this index. Bid buttons are live eBay auctions on the exact card (verified title, soonest close with bids first, refreshed every 15 minutes) — bids, not marks. An index is a measurement, not a call.
