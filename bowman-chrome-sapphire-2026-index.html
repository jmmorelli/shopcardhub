Set index · pre-launch

BCS26 · 2026 Bowman Chrome Sapphire

Dropped on Topps.com (EQL) Wednesday, Oct 7 at 9 am PT, $599.99 a box; Topps has not published a ship date. This is the sapphire edition of September's 2026 Bowman Chrome, not June's 2026 Bowman Sapphire. Like every Bowman index it is based at its release month (100 = Oct 2026); the first level prints once enough cards have real, dated sales, and nothing is printed before then.
PRE
first mark: the Monday or Thursday after the screen fills

What it will track

Main lineThe 33 Chrome Sapphire Prospect Autographs (CSPA) whose September Chrome auto is the player's 1st Bowman auto. A sapphire edition is the same card in sapphire, so each one mirrors the player's September 1st Bowman auto (the 2025 Chrome Sapphire checklist marked its autos 1st; Topps' 2026 checklist marks no 1sts, so the logo on these cards is confirmed from card images as copies surface). The checklist's 34th auto, BSPA-EHA Eric Hartman, is left out: his 1st Bowman auto is May's 2026 Bowman CPA-EHA. Sapphire Selections autos are an insert and are left out.
Sub-indexThe sapphire base Chrome Prospects (BCP-151 to 250, no autograph), tracked as their own line for the non-auto buyer. 72 of the 100 are the player’s 1st Bowman Chrome; the other 28, Holliday BCP-209 and Arquette BCP-174 among them, are returning cards with no 1st logo.
Color ladderBase /199, Green /99, Gold /50, Orange /25, Black /10, Red /5 and Padparadscha 1/1 (print runs per Checklist Insider; base /199 and Green /99 confirmed by a second source).

How it goes live

  • All 33 Chrome Sapphire 1st autos are in the universe. A card enters the basket once it has 6 or more clean dated sales in 30 days.
  • Priced from sold comps only, never asks, and never from the regular Chrome card: a sapphire auto sells at its own price. 100 = the release month (Oct 2026), the Bowman rule since Oct 1; re-marked Monday and Thursday once live.
  • Boxes ship after the online sale, so the first sales may take a few weeks. Launch pricing is recorded, not smoothed away, and the chart will show it.

Player break math · 2026 Bowman Chrome Sapphire Edition Hobby

What a player spot holds in autos

In a player break you buy one prospect's slot and get every card of his that the case yields. A 10-box case holds about 9.8 Chrome Sapphire Prospect Autographs across 34 players, so any one player averages 0.29 autos per case, about one every 3.5 cases. No Chrome Sapphire auto has a verified sale yet, so the table shows the odds only; the dollar column appears with the first sold marks.

Most spots come up empty on autos and a few win big: the average is carried by rare hits. Every box also carries three sapphire parallels; non-auto cards are not counted in the figures below. Auto parallels run Green /99, Gold /50, Orange /25, Black /10, Red /5 and Padparadscha 1/1 (print runs per Checklist Insider); each is counted as one auto, not valued.
