Set index · pre-launch

BF26 · 2026 Bowman Football

Streets Wednesday, Sep 30. Like every Bowman index it is based at its release month (100 = Sep 2026); the first level prints once enough cards have real, dated sales, and nothing is printed before then.
PRE
first mark: the Monday or Thursday after the screen fills

What it will track

Main lineThe 1st Bowman Chrome Autos: the college prospects' first Bowman autographs, the football version of what BOW26 tracks for baseball.
Sub-indexNFL rookie Chrome autos, tracked as their own line so a rookie's Sunday never gets mistaken for the prospect market.
Tier ladder/99, /50, /25, /10 and /5, each priced as a multiple of the same player's base auto. 1/1 SuperFractors are left out: one copy cannot make a tier.

How it goes live

  • Every card in the checklist is in the universe. A card enters the basket once it has 6 or more clean dated sales in 30 days.
  • Priced from sold comps only, never asks. 100 = the release month (Sep 2026), the Bowman rule since Oct 1; re-marked Monday and Thursday once live.
  • Release-week sales run hot. Launch pricing is recorded, not smoothed away, and the chart will show it.
