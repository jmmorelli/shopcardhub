Legend & Methodology

SCOPE — the secret tier: all 49 beyond-base slots (#132-180) - 32 SIRs (the full Eeveelution run), 12 URs, 5 Hyper Rares. Universe = 49, fully priced. Pokémon pull rates differ across rarity tiers, so no equal-supply claim is made — this is a price-weighted chase-basket index, plainly. · WEIGHT — price ÷ basket value (priced cards only). · PRICE — sold comps only, dated per row; never asks, never blended, never estimated. · FULL METHODOLOGY — solds vs asks vs engine marks vs proxies, the one-page read: /how-prices-work · INDEX LEVEL — Σ(sold mark × weight) ÷ divisor (56.638097), set so that 100 = Jan 2025 (release month): the level IS the cumulative return since then. Rebased Sep 30, 2026 from the original Aug 24 launch base (every level × 0.7565; shape and % moves unchanged). Months before the live marks are reconstructed from PriceCharting's monthly ungraded price history with today's basket, labelled on the chart. The divisor adjusts (and is logged) whenever the basket composition changes, so only prices move the level. 25% CAP — since Oct 7, 2026 no card weighs more than 25% of the index: the cap is re-applied at every Monday mark as a logged divisor adjustment, so applying it never moves the level (a 25.0% weight is a capped card). · ★ — Track in your Vault (free, no account): Hunting or I Own It. · NO CALLS — ShopCardHub retired Pokémon price calls on Aug 18, 2026 (graded record: no edge). An index is a measurement, not a recommendation.
