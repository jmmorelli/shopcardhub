Our Analytics Methodology

Our Bowman Bangers system tracks MiLB Pipeline Top 100 movement, weekly stat acceleration vs. peers, and Chrome auto 30-day price trends to flag buy/watch/hold signals. When a player's stats jump faster than their card price — or vice versa — we flag it. The June read on this page — McGonigle's Chrome Auto flagged BUY at +35.8% in 30 days, Wetherholt's Auto down −16.3% — is kept as the original record and is not restated as current. Where those calls stand now is in each player's card rows below, on Sep 16, 2026 sold comps. Graded figures appear only where we read a dated sale record: SportsCardsPro’s grade ladder is partly modelled from comparable cards and is never published here as a sale. Raw card prices on this page are SOLD comps from the SportsCardsPro sold-price index; active listings and asks are never presented as sales.
