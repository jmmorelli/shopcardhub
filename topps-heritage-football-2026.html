2026 Topps Heritage Football — At a Glance

Heritage takes an old Topps design and puts today's players on it. This year it is 1976 Topps Football: thick borders, rounded corners and the football-shaped team nameplate. The set mixes paper base cards with Chrome parallels, short-printed photo variations and autographs.

What This Means

The Investor Read

The presale asks sit near Flagship's sold price. The cheapest single Heritage hobby box on eBay was $175.00 on Oct 3, and most asks clustered around $200 to $210. Flagship Football hobby boxes sold for a $205.00 median in late September. Buyers are pricing the two sets about the same before a single Heritage box has opened.

Retail asks are well above list. The lowest mega ask was $65.00 against a $49.99 list price, and the lowest blaster ask was $39.75 against $24.99. If you can buy at a store on release, that gap is the premium you skip. If you buy presale, you pay it up front.

Base cards are not the play. A raw Mendoza base rookie from Flagship sells for about $1.63. Heritage boxes earn their price from the one hit, the Chrome parallels and the short-printed image variations, and that is where any first-week spread will show.

What we do not know. We do not know what a Heritage box will sell for once boxes are in hand, or which rookies will lead. First sold comps usually appear within days of a street date. This page will get dated Heritage box and card medians after October 21. Until then, every 2026 Heritage figure here is an ask or a list price.
