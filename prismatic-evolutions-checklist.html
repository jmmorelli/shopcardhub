How the estimate works

The count is simply the rows you tick as Have, out of the 180 cards on this sheet (every card pokemontcg.io lists for the set — the 131-card printed set plus the 49 secret rares, #132–180).

The value is a range on purpose. Each card’s number is our latest recent-sales mark for the grade you picked — the median of dated eBay sales, the same figure the PRIS25 index page shows, refreshed every week. We add those up for your Have rows (times quantity) and show 10% under to 10% over that sum, with the date of the newest sale in it. Cards with no recent sales — or no recent sales in the grade you picked — say so and are left out, and the bar tells you how many were left out. Nothing here is an appraisal or an offer. Today the numbers cover the secret rares (#132–180, the cards the PRIS25 index tracks). Base-set cards (#1–131) show “no recent sales” and stay out of the range until we track them.

Saved on this device. Ticks live in your browser (no account). Have also puts the card in your Vault as My cards and Want as Hunting, so the two agree; untick and it leaves. Export CSV keeps a copy; Import CSV brings it back on another device or browser — it adds to what is ticked here and never clears anything. Still need lists every card you have not ticked, each with its eBay search.
