Question Desk · Is Pokémon 151 worth it

Pokémon 151 is worth it as singles, not as packs

Buy the card you want. The five chase cards sum to $716.25 on dated sales, and only one of them — Charizard ex 199/165 at $328.00 — sells for more than the cheapest sealed Booster Bundle asks (lowest ask $169.00, Oct 1). Opening packs to chase a card costs more than buying it.

Want one card? Buy it as a single. Every chase card below has 30 recent sales, so the price is a real one.

Want to open packs? Do it for fun, not for value. One card in 207 sells for more than a bundle asks.

Want to hold sealed? The cheapest bundle asks 6.3 times its 2023 shelf price. That is the run so far; we make no call on the next one.

Fresh read, Oct 1, 2026: the answer is the same as on Sep 26. Charizard raw moved from $342.63 to $328.00; the cheapest clean bundle ask is $169.00. The SV151 index, which prices all 207 cards, stood at 170.92 at its Sep 28 mark, with 100 = Sep 2023 (release).

The arithmetic
Booster Bundle lowest ask $169.00 ÷ 6 packs = $28.17 per pack (Oct 1 asks)
Bundle lowest ask $169.00 ÷ MSRP $26.99 = 6.3x the shelf price it launched at in 2023
Cards in the set whose sold median tops that ask: 1 of 207 (Charizard ex 199/165; every other card sat under $169 at the index's Sep 28 mark)
A $169.00 bundle needs the one card in 207 to beat buying that card outright for $328.00.

What This Means

Reading the number

Buy the card, not the pack. Every chase card here shows 30 raw sales inside the last one to four weeks (the tab caps at 30), so the price you see is close to the price you pay. The cheapest clean Booster Bundle ask, $169.00, is 6.3x MSRP, and that premium is paid to whoever held sealed product for three years, not to you. If you want the Charizard, $328.00 buys it; $169.00 buys six packs with no promise of it.

As a sealed hold, the run so far is on the record; the next one is not. The bundle went from $26.99 to a $169.00 lowest ask in three years. The ETB shelf is four listings deep from $550, which is a number we report and do not lean on. Whether sealed keeps climbing depends on the print run and the reprint schedule, and neither is on this page because we could not read either from a source we would cite.

The grading spread is where 151 rewards work. Charizard ex PSA 10 at $1,400.00 is 4.3x raw on 29 dated sales; a PSA 9 at $355.00 is barely over raw. Charmander's PSA 10 at $605.00 is 6.7x raw, but on 8 dated sales, so read it as a thin market, not a target.

As an investment, the set sits about 1.7x its release month; sealed asks have run further. SV151 read 170.92 at its Sep 28 mark, with 100 = Sep 2023 (release). That path is a monthly reconstruction until Sep 25, 2026, built on today's basket, so read it as a shape, not a return anyone banked: a low of 83.39 in Apr 2024, a high of 203.19 in May 2026, and the live marks since Sep 25 below that high. Over the same three years the cheapest bundle ask went to 6.3x MSRP. Neither number says what happens next.
