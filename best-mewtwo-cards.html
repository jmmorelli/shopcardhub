Gengar's Collector's Desk · No. 3 · Mewtwo

The Mewtwo cards worth collecting

Mewtwo was the first Pokémon that felt dangerous, and the card game has treated it that way for 27 years: a Base Set holo, the Shining, the Rocket version, and now a Team Rocket's Mewtwo ex that is the heaviest card in its set. These are the ones I'd keep, from a $3 holo to a Shadowless slab.

The desk notes

How to collect Mewtwo without getting burned

Three Base Set Mewtwos

1st Edition, Shadowless and Unlimited are three separate assets with separate PSA labels, separate population reports and separate price histories. Our indices track each print as its own card and every link on this page searches for the exact print on its row.

Two Team Rocket's Mewtwo ex cards

Destined Rivals (2025) and Ascended Heroes (2026) each have a Team Rocket's Mewtwo ex Special Illustration Rare. They are different cards with different numbers, and a search that drops the set name will mix them.

Modern value lives in the 10

On modern Special Illustration Rares a PSA 9 sells for about what the raw card does; the premium is almost entirely in the PSA 10. On the vintage holos the gap is far wider, and a raw vintage mark is a typical raw sale in any condition, not a near-mint price.

On grading

PSA 10 is the hobby's price reference. TAG grades by machine-measured imaging with human review and publishes a full defect report for every card, which is why we link TAG slabs beside PSA on every row. We think measured grading is where the hobby is heading — our view, not a price claim. How TAG grading works →

Where every number came from

Method

Raw is each card's sold mark in its own ShopCardHub set index: the median of its clean ungraded sold comps (PriceCharting completed sales) over the trailing 30 days, 90 days on the 1st Edition and Shadowless indices, re-marked Monday and Thursday — the same number as the index table and the card page. PSA 9, PSA 10 and TAG 10 are read from dated completed sales whose own listing title names that grade: the median of 3+ sales in 30 days, else 2+ in 90 days. Fewer than that is no mark; a single sale is shown as one dated sale, never as a price. A raw vintage copy can be in any condition, so a raw mark on a 1999 card is a typical raw sale, not a near-mint price. Marks on this page were read between Sep 30, 2026 and Oct 5, 2026. eBay links are searches for the exact print on each row, with our affiliate tag — live listings, never a price.
